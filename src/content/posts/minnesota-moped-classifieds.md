A little while back I setup a classified ad system on the [Casserollers](http://casserollers.com "Casserollers") website.The goal was/is to have something that makes it easy for people to buy/sell/trade moped parts locally and not have to worry about shipping, getting scammed or having to filter through craigslist/ebay. Not that it's impossible to get scammed/burnt once you take out the shipping variable, but it should help out a bunch.

Anywho if you're in the area and you'd like to place buy/sell some moped stuff please give it a shot.
