This last weekend I went down to [Sport Wheels](http://sportwheel.com/ "Sport Wheels") in Jordan, MN, bought some stuff and snapped some pictures while I was at it. It's a pretty cool place. It's basically a gigantic motorcycle salvage, but you can also find a little bit of atv, snowmobile and moped stuff there.

My dad actually turned me on to them, he's been an avid motorcyclist for most of his adult life and has bought several bikes from them over the years. When I was looking to get a motorcycle this winter he told me I should check out what they have. In addition to being a salvage yard they also sell running motorcycles. They have a selection of bikes that are in running condition that they've either fixed up or recieved as trade ins and stuff like that. I ended up picking up my [1982 Honda Nighthawk 650](/2011/03/my-first-motorcycle/ "My First Motorcycle") from them and it's been a great bike.

Super nice people too! I highly recommend them!
