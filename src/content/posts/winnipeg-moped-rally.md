This last weekend Myself and a bunch of Casserollers made a visit to Winnipeg to rally with our Canadian friends. Dru and Heather were gracious hosts and let us setup a shantytown of tents in their backyard. Weather was a little drizzly, but didn't stop us from getting some great rides in. We ate Poutine, played dice, did some parts swapping, Jake won a snowboard and Nick won the raffle bike (Motobecane 50L). I'm so glad we made the trip and I'm looking forward to seeing these guys again at our rally in a few weeks.
