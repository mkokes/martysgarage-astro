This past weekend the wife and I went to The Summerai's 'Just The Tip' rally in Green Bay, WI. I've always wanted to go, but for one reason or another I had scheduling conflicts every year. Well this year was the year the stars aligned and I could finally go.

We got off to a little bit of a rocky start, [wifeped](/vehicle/wifeped/ "Wifeped") seized hard Friday night on the way back from the bar. I took it on some test runs when we first got there and it was running perfectly. I'm thinking something mechanical not necessarily tuning related gave way. I haven't opened up that Christmas present yet so no final diagnosis. She was cool with just driving my car and meeting us at the campsite though.

Other than that it was an absolute blast, we started out in green bay rode about 100 miles to the tip of door county camped overnight and then rode another 100 miles or so back the next day. Beautiful scenery, great roads , met a bunch of cool people and had an ridiculous amount of fun. I'm definitely going to try and make it happen again next year.

Here's a handful of the picture I took during the adventure, I did a bunch of video too, but it's going to take me a little bit to go through it all so that will be in a future post or something.
