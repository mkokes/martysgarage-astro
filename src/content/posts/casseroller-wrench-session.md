I had a bunch of the guys over to do some winter wrenching this weekend. We got a bunch of stuff done in preparation for summer blasting.

A little bit of leakdown testing, Kim's hobbit engine was air tight, Brian's Gila E50 not so much. Jimbo did some porting on his Motobecane AV10 Polini setup (that thing is going to be nuts!). Jed did some chopping grinding and painting.
