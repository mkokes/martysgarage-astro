I didn't bring my camera with me for our first ride, but here's the meetup on day two. It was a pretty awesome ride, we went from [Pizza Luce](http://www.pizzaluce.com/locations/?loc=uptown "Pizza Luce Uptown") in uptown Minneapolis to [Maynard](http://maynards-excelsior.com/ "Maynards")s on Lake Minnetonka and then down to one of the rider's houses in Eden Prairie and then back up to Minneapolis. Nice long ride, good weather and beautiful scenery.
