As with most personal blogs posting is rarely consistent. This isn't a job (if it was I shoulda been canned a long time ago), life gets busy, projects are set aside, sometimes I just want to tinker and not have to worry about documenting what I'm doing.

So rather than checking my site for updates there's always my [Facebook](http://facebook.com/martysgarage "Marty's Garage Facebook Page") and [Twitter](http://twitter.com/martysgarage "Marty's Garage Twitter Page") pages that get updated whenever I push anything new out. There's also my [Youtube Channel](https://www.youtube.com/user/MartyksGarage "Marty's Garage Youtube Channel") if you're just into video. Now I've added an [email newsletter](/newsletter-signup/ "Newsletter Signup"), no spam or anything, it'll just send out a notification whenever I put up anything new no more that once a day. So if email is your go to thing, here you go.
