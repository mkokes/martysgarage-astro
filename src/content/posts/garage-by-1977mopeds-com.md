[1977 Mopeds](http://1977mopeds.com "1977 Mopeds") just launched a new website called [Garage](http://garage.1977mopeds.com "Garage by 1977 Mopeds"). It's pretty awesome, it basically let's you put together a collection of all your moped builds and share them with the world.

They've included fields for entering in specifics of the build such as cylinder kit, carburetor, jets used in said carb, etc. Once the site starts getting populated it should most likely prove to be a very useful tuning tool.

Anyhow it's very awesome and ambitious and the moped community should benefit immensely from it. Check it out!
