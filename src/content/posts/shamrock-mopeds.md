Hey Everybody!

For the last couple of months my posts have become sporadic to say the least. Now that things have progressed a little bit I figured I'd fill you in on what I've been up to. This summer my friend josh and I started a little side business fixing and selling mopeds and scooters with the help of [this content](https://www.unsecuredloans4u.co.uk/bad-credit-loans/) to make my budget enough to get a good start. Although time consuming it's been a lot of fun and a great experience so far.

Right now we're just working out of my garage, but eventually we hope to open our own store in the Minneapols, MN area. If you'd like to learn more about all the fun moped stuff we're doing take a peek at our [website](http://shamrockmopeds.com). I'm really liking working on mopeds, it's so nice to be able to pull the entire engine off of the machine and just throw it on my work bench.

[![](/wp-content/uploads/2010/10/Shamrock-Mopeds.png "Shamrock Mopeds")](http://shamrockmopeds.com)

As far as the MG goes I still have every intention of restoring it and at this point I've got plenty of parts and work to do without spending $$$. At this point I'm figuring our moped work will start to slow down once the snow starts to fall here in Minnesota and then it'll be MG time. I'll be sure to start posting updates when I have something to share.
