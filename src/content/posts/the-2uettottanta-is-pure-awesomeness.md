Alfa Romeo's unpronounceable(I'm just going to make a bunch of T noises) new concept car has stolen my heart! I sure hope this ends up in America some day. It makes me think of what a modern day MG B would look like if they still made them today.

One thing I know for sure is I'm definitely going to have to take some ques from the interior of this car when it comes to doing my own custom interior. I really like how the dash wraps around onto the doors.
