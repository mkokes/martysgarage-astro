The tail end of last year I traded my [green Motobecane](/vehicle/oppenheimer/ "Oppenheimer") for a Peugeot project that fellow [Casseroller](/vehicle/oppenheimer/) Brian Rogers was working on. I've been stockpiling parts here and there to round it it until I got some my other projects taken care of before I jumped in on it.

![Peugeot 103 Build](/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/jpeg.jpg)

*Peugeot 103 frame with a crossbar welded in and a yamaha something or other seat pan*

![Box of Parts](/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/1972776_10151888507722237_872524712_o.jpg)

*Box O' Parts*

Well I'm on [transmission repair cape coral fl](http://www.karrysautomotive.com/services/engine-transmission/) now, I'm taking my first stab at a liquid cooled frenchie this should be fun.

I'm going to be running the 50cc W port polini kit with a polini case, you can see the other parts going into it on [it's project page](/vehicle/viridian/ "Viridian"). I'll be doing more posts on the mechanical setup, but for now I'll start out with what's obviously the most important part of any build, THE HEADLIGHT.

[![Headlight Fairing](/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/jpeg1.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/jpeg1.jpg)

I picked up this ridiculously [awesome headlight fairing](https://www.treatland.tv/black-dual-cyclops-headlight-fairing-p/headlight-fairing-440601.htm "moped headlight fairing") thing from treats. It's made for a dirtbike or something with forks that have a little wider triple tree than a moped so I had to do a little bit of bracket making to make it play nice. It's brackets are roughly 7.5" apart where as the headlight bracket for my suzuki k10 forks is more like 5". [![10253062\_10151945094197237\_460057053\_o](/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/10253062_10151945094197237_460057053_o.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/10253062_10151945094197237_460057053_o.jpg)

I cut a few pieces of steel to the proper length and welded some tubing to them to act as a spacer. Side note those spring are some beefier [k10 springs](http://bit.ly/1qvLOeq "Beefy K10 Springs Thread") I picked up off of carlos on moped army.

![headlight brackets and k10 springs](/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/10253042_10151946003167237_2065168879_o.jpg)

*Headlight brackets and k10 springs*

Powder coating stuff cause when you have a powder coating gun you want to powdercoat everything.

![Powder coating operation](/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/10260604_10151947809292237_1737905369_o.jpg)

*Poor man's powder coating operation*

The finished product, I screwed up the cure time on one of the springs a little bit so the finish looks a little different, but I don't really mind.

![Powder Coated Hardware](/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/10177186_10151948613132237_1396773486_o.jpg)

*Fuzzy pic of the finished brackets and springs*

![Headlight Brackets](/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/898689_10151948621397237_60587577_o.jpg)

*Brackets mounted up*

It worked out quite well I'd say, with this headlight setup I think it's safe to say I'm obviously going to win every race ever.
