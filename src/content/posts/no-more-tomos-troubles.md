Well as I mentioned in a [previous post](/2011/06/tomos-troubles/ "Tomos Troubles") I ran into some trouble with my 1995 Tomos Targa. I wasn't really sure if it was and issue with the carb being jetted a little too lean of if my problems were ignition related. Turns out it was the latter since after I replaced the CDI on it with the [Treats e50 CDI adventure pack](http://www.treatland.tv/puch-za50-e50-CDI-complete-pack-p/puch-cdi-complete.htm "Treats e50 CDI") I was back in action.

It was a little tricky since I lost the woodruff key so a little trial and error was needed (A LOT), but in the end it all worked out.
