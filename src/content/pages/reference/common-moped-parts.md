Here's a list of parts I've thrown together of some of the more generic moped parts, mainly to make it easier to buy parts locally when it's more convenient.  

## Engine Bearings and Seals

  

### Puch e50

  

  
-   3 x 6203 Bearings
  
-   1 x 6203NR Bearing
  
-   1 x 22x40x7 seal
  
-   2 x 17x40x7 seal
  

  

### Motobecane

  

  
-   2 x 16x42x13 - 6302/16 (FAG)
  
-   2 x 15x21x4 seal (SPI, FAG)
  

  

### Tomos

  

  
-   2 - 6203 Bearings (a35/a55)
  
-   2 - L17A Bearings (A3)
  
-   1 - 608 Bearing
  
-   1 - 6000 Bearing
  
-   1 - 6201 Bearing
  
-   1 - 6006 Bearing
  
-   2 - 17x35x7 Seals (crank)
  
-   1 - 16x24x7 Seal (pedal shaft)
  
-   1 - 35x47x7 Seal (output shaft)
  

  

### Honda Hobbit

  

  
-   2 x 6004 C3 Bearings
  
-   2 x 15x25.5x7 Seals
